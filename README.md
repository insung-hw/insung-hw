### Hi, I'm Insung 👋

I explore how data and AI can support business decisions—from understanding customers to forecasting markets and building useful tools.

My projects connect **analysis, modeling, and practical applications**, with a focus on customer behavior, market forecasting, and AI-assisted marketing.

<br />

### Selected Projects

#### 🛒 [AI Target Marketing Assistant](https://github.com/insung-hw/dunnhumby)

An AI marketing assistant that turns shopping data into personalized product recommendations and campaign messages.

- Combines household purchase profiles with retrieved reference documents to draft campaign briefs.
- Includes automated checks, supporting evidence, and a review interface with JSON / Excel exports.

<sub>Python · SQL · BigQuery · Streamlit · LangChain · Gemini / Claude</sub>

<br />

#### 🎟️ [Ponpare Customer Analytics](https://github.com/insung-hw/ponpare-market-finding)

Customer segmentation and purchase analysis to identify growth opportunities for a Japanese coupon marketplace.

- Uses RFM segmentation and cohort analysis to understand customer behavior.
- Connects purchase patterns and category preferences to customer activation proposals.

<sub>Python · SQL · pandas · Seaborn · BigQuery</sub>

<br />

#### 📈 [Neodymium Price Forecasting](https://github.com/insung-hw/neodymium-price-forcasting)

Machine learning forecasts and export-control scenarios to explore neodymium prices and supply chain risk.

- Compares tree-based forecasting models across economic regimes and variable sets.
- Led topic selection, data collection, modeling, and report writing in a two-member research team.

<sub>Python · Darts · XGBoost · LightGBM · CatBoost · scikit-learn</sub>
